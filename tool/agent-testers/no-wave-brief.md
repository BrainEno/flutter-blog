# Brief: No Wave (reading and writing, account on a test backend)

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first: its tools, rules and final report all apply.
Only the goal and the scope change.

- The app talks to a **test** no-wave backend. Use only the test account in
  your persona's `email` field or `success+<persona>@simulator.amazonses.com`;
  never a real address. Decline any prompt to send crash reports.
- The interface ships in Simplified Chinese and English. A device in any other
  language should fall back to one of them: report a screen that mixes the
  two, or shows a raw key or an English word inside Chinese copy, once.
- Your goal instead, as your persona:
  1. Open an article from the home feed, read to the end, and change the text
     size from the reader's toolbar.
  2. Find the comments under the article. As a guest, try to reply; then sign
     in (or sign up) and post a comment, reply to it, edit it, and delete it.
     Check that each thread reads as its own block, that replies are clearly
     attached to what they answer, and that the author of the article is
     marked when they answer.
  3. Save the article, then find it again in the Saved tab.
  4. Start the read-aloud from the article, scroll while it plays, and stop it.
- Also look at: search (an article, then an author), a category, the profile
  page and its settings (theme, language), and what happens when you turn on
  airplane mode in the middle of reading and come back.
- Out of scope: the Notion import and export, publishing a real article,
  account deletion, and changing the account e-mail.
